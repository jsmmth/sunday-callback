Sunday privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Sunday is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's Mac. It is not a service: there is no Sunday server, no accounts with us, and it is used only by the person who set it up.

What Sunday accesses

  • Gmail: reads messages to answer questions, saves drafts, marks messages read, archives and stars them. It sends, replies to or moves a message to the Bin only after the owner confirms that specific message on screen.
  • Google Calendar: reads events, and adds, changes or cancels an event only after the owner confirms it on screen.
  • Bank accounts (through a regulated open-banking provider): reads balances and transactions. It cannot make payments.

Where data goes

Sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner's Mac. Email, calendar and bank data is processed on that Mac and cached there. To answer a question, the relevant text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API under Anthropic's commercial terms, which do not allow it to be used for training. Spoken replies are generated by ElevenLabs. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or sent anywhere else.

Google user data

Sunday's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the assistant's features to its owner, is not transferred to others except as described above, and is never used for advertising or to train general AI models.

Removing access

Disconnect any account in Sunday › Settings › Accounts, which deletes its token from the Keychain. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This page

This site only hosts this policy and the page that returns you to Sunday after you approve a bank. It stores nothing, uses no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.
